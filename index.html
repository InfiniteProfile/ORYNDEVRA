The reality that was never meant to exist. Every positive whole number is a being, and each one is always the same being.

About this universe

ORYNDEVRA has no space, no galaxies and no ordinary civilizations. Axiom Fields decide what can exist. Existence Zones hold beings without needing distance. Origin Patterns explain why something begins. Bodies may be made of memory, silence or debt, and time is a material rather than a river.

Search an exact ID of any length to jump straight to that being. Nothing is stored: each profile is rebuilt from its ID.
